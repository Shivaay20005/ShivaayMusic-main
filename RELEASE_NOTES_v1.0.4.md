# 🎵 ShivaayMusic v1.0.4

> **Network-Resilient Playback, Advanced Storage Control, and Visual Excellence.**

---

## ✨ What's New

### ⚡ Smart Pre-fetch (Audio Buffering)
- **Zero Lag Experience**: Implemented an intelligent pre-fetching engine that buffers the next 1-2 songs while the current track is playing.
- **Network Resilience**: Specifically designed for unstable networks (Metro, Tunnels, Weak Wi-Fi).
- **Gapless Transitions**: Enjoy seamless transitions between tracks as if they were already on your device.

### 🧹 Advanced Cache Management
- **Real-time Storage Tracking**: Now see exactly how much space ShivaayMusic is using directly in the settings.
- **Smart Clear Cache**: One-tap cleaning that intelligently deletes old cached files while protecting your active playback and offline downloads.
- **Periodic Cleanup**: Background tasks ensure your storage usage stays optimized without manual intervention.

### 💎 UI/UX & Visual Hierarchy
- **Settings Overhaul**: Redesigned category headers with primary color accents and improved typography for better scanability.
- **Faded Subtitles**: Applied subtle opacity to descriptions to make primary titles pop and reduce visual clutter.
- **Search Refinement**: Fixed layout shifts in the search screen, ensuring the voice search integration fits perfectly into the premium aesthetic.

---

## 🛠️ Performance & Stability

- **ABI Split Support**: Now distributing specialized APKs (`arm64-v8a`, `armeabi-v7a`, `x86_64`) for smaller install sizes and maximum performance on your specific device.
- **Permanent Signing**: Migrated to a dedicated keystore system. Future updates will be seamless without "Package Conflict" issues, even if you switch devices.
- **Smart Cleanup Engine**: Fixed potential crashes during cache deletion by skipping files currently in use by the audio engine.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. **UNINSTALL** any previous version of ShivaayMusic to avoid package conflicts.
2. Install the **1.0.4 APK** matching your device architecture (arm64-v8a is recommended for modern phones).
